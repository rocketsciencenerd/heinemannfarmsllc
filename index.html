Our Story

Honest Work,
Honest Craft

At Heinemann Farms LLC, we believe in doing things right the first time. Whether it's breathing new life into a vintage tractor, hand-crafting a piece of custom woodwork, or coaxing a tired old boat back onto the water — we bring the same pride and precision to every single job.

We're a working farm and shop rooted in Wisconsin. From the land we tend to the machines we restore, everything we do carries a commitment to quality that outlasts trends and seasons.

What We Do

Six Trades,
One Standard

From mechanical restoration to living landscapes — every service carries the same commitment to craft and durability.

01

Tractor Repair & Restoration

From routine maintenance to full restorations, we keep your iron running right. Old Farmalls, John Deeres, Olivers — we know these machines inside and out.

02

Vintage Auto Restoration

Classic cars deserve classic attention. We restore vintage and antique automobiles with care — mechanical work, bodywork, and everything in between.

03

Custom Woodworking

Hand-crafted furniture, signs, cabinetry, and more. We work with local hardwoods to create pieces built to last generations.

04

Boat Repair & Restoration

Hull work, engine service, fiberglass repair, interior woodwork — we get your boat lake-ready and keep it there. Old wood boats are a specialty.

05

Tree Planting & Care

We plant, establish, and care for trees — from windbreaks and orchard rows to shade trees and privacy plantings. Long-term thinking for the land.

06

Gardening & Landscaping

Vegetable gardens, perennial beds, lawn care, and more. We'll help you grow something beautiful — from first planting through harvest and beyond.
