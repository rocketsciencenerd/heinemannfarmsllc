Our Story

Wood, Grain,
& Good Work

At Heinemann Farms LLC, every piece starts with the wood itself — the grain, the figure, the character that no two boards share. We work with walnut, maple, cherry, and burl to build boxes, chests, and furniture that are meant to be passed down.

Based in Wisconsin, we take custom orders and make one-of-a-kind pieces. Whether it's a jewelry box as a wedding gift or a furniture commission for your home, we bring the same patience and precision to every project.

Solid Hardwoods

Walnut, maple, cherry, and burl — no plywood, no shortcuts.

Hand-Fitted Joinery

Finger joints, dovetails, and mortise & tenon — built to hold.

Made to Order

Every piece is custom — built for you, from scratch.
