Our Story

Wood, Grain,
& Good Work

At Heinemann Farms LLC, every piece starts with the wood itself — the grain, the figure, the character that no two boards share. We work with walnut, maple, cherry, and bird's eye maple to build boxes, chests, and furniture that are meant to be passed down.

Based in Wisconsin, we take custom orders and make one-of-a-kind pieces. Whether it's a jewelry box as a wedding gift or a furniture commission for your home, we bring the same patience and precision to every project.

Solid Hardwoods

Walnut, maple, cherry, and bird's eye maple — no plywood, no shortcuts.

Hand-Fitted Joinery

Finger joints, dovetails, and mortise & tenon — built to hold.

Made to Order

Every piece is custom — built for you, from scratch.

What We Make

Nine Specialties,
One Standard

Every piece is built from solid hardwood, fitted by hand, and finished to last a lifetime — or longer.

Jewelry & Keepsake Boxes

Our signature piece. Handcrafted from walnut, maple, and cherry with flox-lined interiors, brass hardware, and hand-fitted finger joints.

Heirloom Chests

Larger keepsake chests and treasure boxes built to be passed down. We match wood species and design to your story and your family.

Custom Furniture

Tables, benches, shelving, and more — built from solid hardwood to your dimensions, your finish, your style. No particleboard, ever.

Gift & Occasion Pieces

Weddings, retirements, anniversaries — a hand-crafted wooden piece is a gift that outlasts any occasion. Custom engraving available.

Home Décor & Signs

Serving boards, wall art, carved signs, and decorative pieces. We bring the warmth of real wood into every room.

Custom Commissions

Have something in mind that doesn't fit a category? Bring your idea. We love a challenge — the stranger the brief, the better the story.

Camper & RV Interiors

Custom-fit dinette tables, fold-down surfaces, and built-in woodwork designed to make your camper feel like home. Measured to fit your rig.

Countertops & Cutting Boards

Butcher block countertops, end-grain cutting boards, and stove covers built from hardwood. Functional, food-safe, and built to outlast the kitchen.

Custom Shelving

Built-in shelving, cabinetry, and storage solutions crafted from solid wood. Designed around your space — no off-the-shelf dimensions, no compromises.
